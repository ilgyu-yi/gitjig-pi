- The dispatcher's run bound is reachable from both registered surfaces: `gitjig_dispatch` advertises `timeoutMs` and `/review` reads an optional leading `timeoutMs=<n>` token, where before the option was declared and threaded to the executor but set by no call site, so every dispatch ran at the 120-second default and no real review delegate could complete. A present-but-inadmissible bound — non-numeric, zero, negative, NaN or infinite — refuses on a fixed content-free cause rather than resolving to a bound the caller did not ask for, and at the tool surface that refusal lands its own audit record; an absent bound stays legal and takes the default.
