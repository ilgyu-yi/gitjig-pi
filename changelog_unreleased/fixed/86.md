- The egress pattern loader measures a stated list of constructs outside the POSIX-ERE and RegExp common subset, instead of calling RegExp compilability a subset check: a JS-only construct now refuses the load rather than compiling at the publish reader and diverging at the commit-time one. Neutralization no longer wraps a close pair whose separator crosses a blank line — the wrap could not form a code span across a paragraph break, so the reference shipped live wearing backticks — and the `GH-N` form is neutralized in either case, where before the lowercase spelling the platform also autolinks passed through untouched. A conformance case now fails loud if a `$`-anchored pattern is armed, since body lines keep their carriage return and such a pattern would silently under-match every CRLF-composed line. (#86)
