- The local hook tier's source fold decides its window by counting live `githook_source` frames on the shell's own call stack, instead of keeping a depth counter a sourced file could reach by name — closing an accidental corruption the counter carried. That counter was initialized at file scope with no re-entry guard, so a helper that re-sourced the prelude — directly, transitively through a sibling, or from a nested source — reset it inside an open window and drove it below its floor, leaving the next window with no trap behind it. Both directions of harm were measured against a tree carrying the counter: where the later helper exited non-zero the commit was refused with no stderr line and no record, the wedged hook with nothing printed; where it exited zero the commit was created with no line and no record, so that helper's arm never ran and nothing said so. The frame count folds open in both, with its line and its record. Three other accidental shapes were measured not to move the counter. Reaching the window on purpose still forges an allow, and stands as an enumerated residual. (#71)
